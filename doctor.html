      Doctor Career Roadmap

      India mein doctor banne ka general educational pathway.

      01

      10th ke Baad Science Chunein

      11th–12th mein Physics, Chemistry aur Biology (PCB) padhein. Medical admission ki current eligibility check karein.

      02

      NEET-UG ki Taiyari

      MBBS admission ke liye NEET-UG ke current syllabus, eligibility aur counselling rules check karein.

      03

      MBBS Complete Karein

      Recognised medical college mein MBBS ki academic aur required clinical training complete karein.

      04

      Compulsory Internship

      Applicable rules ke according required supervised medical internship complete karein.

      05

      Registration aur Career

      Applicable medical registration requirements poori karke eligible medical roles ke liye apply karein.

      06

      Specialist Banne ka Raasta

      Specialist doctor banne ke liye relevant postgraduate medical course aur uske admission requirements explore karein.

      Course duration, eligibility, registration aur admission rules ko official authorities se verify karein.
