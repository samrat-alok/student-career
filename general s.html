EDUCATION PATHWAY

Understand the academic journey required to enter the field of General Surgery.

Class 10

Build a strong foundation in Science, Mathematics and English. Develop curiosity about the human body and healthcare.

Class 11–12

Choose Physics, Chemistry and Biology (PCB). Prepare for the NEET-UG medical entrance examination.

After Class 12

Qualify NEET-UG and secure admission to an eligible medical college for the MBBS programme.

After MBBS

Complete the compulsory internship and registration requirements, then pursue recognised postgraduate training in General Surgery.

SURGICAL SPECIALISATIONS

Explore different areas of advanced surgical practice.

Gastrointestinal Surgery

Focus on surgical conditions involving the stomach, intestines and digestive system.

Oncosurgery

Work with surgical treatment of cancer and related conditions.

Paediatric Surgery

Focus on surgical conditions affecting infants, children and adolescents.

Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery

Learn about reconstructive procedures, burns care and tissue repair.

Vascular Surgery

Focus on diseases and conditions involving blood vessels.

Minimal Access Surgery

Explore laparoscopic and other minimally invasive surgical techniques.

Breast Surgery

Focus on surgical assessment and treatment of breast conditions.

Endocrine Surgery

Work with surgical conditions involving thyroid, parathyroid and adrenal glands.

ESSENTIAL SKILLS

Medical Knowledge

Understand human anatomy, physiology, pathology and surgical diseases.

Surgical Technique

Develop operative knowledge and technical skills through supervised training.

Clinical Assessment

Learn patient history-taking, physical examination and diagnostic evaluation.

Decision Making

Evaluate clinical situations and make evidence-based treatment decisions.

Emergency Management

Understand the principles of emergency assessment and initial patient stabilisation.

Communication

Explain procedures, risks and treatment plans clearly to patients and families.

Teamwork

Collaborate with anaesthesiologists, nurses and other healthcare professionals.

Professional Ethics

Maintain patient confidentiality, informed consent and professional responsibility.

    CAREER & JOB OPPORTUNITIES

    General Surgeon

    Diagnose and surgically manage a range of conditions requiring operative care.

    Consultant Surgeon

    Provide specialist surgical services in eligible hospitals and healthcare institutions.

    Trauma Surgeon

    Work with surgical teams to manage serious injuries and emergency conditions.

    Laparoscopic Surgeon

    Focus on minimally invasive surgical procedures after appropriate training.

    Medical Educator

    Teach medical students and train future healthcare professionals.

    Clinical Researcher

    Participate in research on surgical techniques, treatments and patient outcomes.
