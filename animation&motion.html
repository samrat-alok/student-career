01 / INTRODUCTION

What Is Animation & Motion Design?

Animation is the process of creating the illusion of movement through a sequence of images or 3D objects. Motion Design uses movement, typography, graphics and visual effects to communicate ideas. Both fields are used in films, games, advertising, entertainment, education and digital products.

🎨 Creativity

Develop characters, visual concepts, stories and artistic ideas.

🎬 Storytelling

Communicate emotions and messages through moving visuals.

⚡ Technology

Use professional animation software, digital tools and rendering.

03 / PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

How Is Animation Created?

Understand the professional animation production pipeline.

01

Concept

Develop the idea, story and creative direction.

02

Script

Write the story, dialogue and scene descriptions.

03

Storyboard

Plan scenes and camera angles using visual sketches.

04

Design & Assets

Create characters, backgrounds, objects and graphics.

05

Animation

Create movement using keyframes, timing and interpolation.

06

Rendering

Generate final images or video from the animation scene.

07

Compositing

Combine layers, effects, backgrounds and visual elements.

08

Editing & Sound

Add music, sound effects, voiceovers and final edits.
