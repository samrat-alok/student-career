What is Biotechnology?

Biotechnology is a scientific field that uses living organisms, cells, biological systems and biological processes to develop useful products and technologies. It has applications in medicine, agriculture, food production, environmental protection and research.

Biotechnology Specializations

Explore different branches of Biotechnology.

🧬 Genetic Engineering

Study and modify genetic material for research and applications.

Topics: DNA, genes, genetic modification.

🔬 Microbiology

Study microorganisms such as bacteria, fungi and viruses.

Topics: Microbial biology, cultures, identification.

🧫 Molecular Biology

Understand biological processes at the molecular level.

Topics: DNA, RNA, proteins, gene expression.

💻 Bioinformatics

Use programming and computational tools to analyse biological data.

Topics: Python, biological databases, genomics.

💊 Medical Biotechnology

Explore biotechnology applications in medicine and healthcare.

Topics: Diagnostics, vaccines, therapeutics.

🌱 Agricultural Biotechnology

Develop biotechnology applications for crops and agriculture.

Topics: Crop improvement, plant tissue culture.

🌍 Environmental Biotechnology

Apply biological processes to environmental protection.

Topics: Bioremediation, waste treatment.

⚗️ Industrial Biotechnology

Use biological systems to manufacture useful industrial products.

Topics: Fermentation, enzymes, bioprocessing.

Biotechnology Career Roadmap

Follow these stages to prepare for a biotechnology career.

01

Build Your Foundation

Learn Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics and basic biological concepts such as cells, DNA and proteins.

02

Learn Core Subjects

Study Genetics, Microbiology, Biochemistry, Molecular Biology, Immunology and Cell Biology.

03

Learn Laboratory & Digital Skills

Understand laboratory safety, research methods, biological data analysis, Python and bioinformatics tools.

04

Build Academic Projects

Work on biological data analysis, research reviews, computational biology and supervised laboratory projects.

05

Internship & Research Experience

Explore opportunities in biotechnology companies, universities, research institutes and laboratories.

06

Prepare for Jobs & Higher Studies

Prepare your resume, research portfolio, technical interviews and relevant entrance examinations.
