      STEP-BY-STEP PLAN

      Your Design Career Roadmap

      Follow these steps to move from beginner to job-ready designer.

      01

      Explore Your Interest

      Choose a design field based on what you enjoy creating.

      02

      Learn the Fundamentals

      Study colors, typography, composition, layout and usability.

      03

      Practice Design Tools

      Start with tools relevant to your field, such as Figma, Canva, Photoshop, Illustrator or Blender.

      04

      Build a Portfolio

      Create 4–6 strong projects and explain the problem and your solution.

      05

      Apply for Internships

      Prepare your resume, portfolio and a short introduction.

      06

      Prepare for Jobs

      Practice portfolio presentations, design tasks and interview questions.
