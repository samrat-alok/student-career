Cardiology Career Roadmap

Roadmap Completion 0%

Develop a strong academic foundation in Science, Mathematics, and English.

Study Physics, Chemistry, and Biology. Prepare for medical entrance examinations.

Prepare for and qualify the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test for undergraduate medical admission.

Complete the prescribed MBBS academic and clinical training at a recognised medical institution.

Gain supervised clinical experience through the required internship and complete applicable registration requirements.

Follow an eligible postgraduate pathway, commonly MD/DNB General Medicine, according to current admission rules.

Complete the required super-speciality training, commonly DM/DrNB Cardiology, subject to current eligibility rules.

Develop clinical expertise, maintain professional registration, and pursue suitable cardiology positions.

    CAREER OPPORTUNITIES

    Career Options in Cardiology

    Clinical Cardiologist

    Diagnose and manage cardiovascular conditions and provide patient care.

    Interventional Cardiologist

    Perform specialised catheter-based cardiovascular procedures after appropriate training.

    Electrophysiologist

    Specialise in heart rhythm disorders and related procedures.

    Academic Cardiologist

    Teach medical students, conduct research, and contribute to medical education.

    Cardiac Researcher

    Work on cardiovascular research, clinical studies, and medical innovation.

    Hospital Consultant

    Work in hospitals, specialised cardiac centres, and healthcare institutions.
