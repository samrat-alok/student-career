What is Electrical Engineering?

Electrical Engineering is a branch of engineering that focuses on the generation, transmission, distribution and application of electrical energy. It also includes electrical machines, control systems, power electronics and renewable energy.

Electrical Engineering Specializations

Explore different fields within Electrical Engineering.

⚡ Power Systems

Generation, transmission and distribution of electrical power.

Topics: Power plants, substations, grid systems.

🔌 Electrical Machines

Design and operation of motors, generators and transformers.

Topics: AC motors, DC motors, transformers.

☀️ Renewable Energy

Use of clean energy sources to generate electricity.

Topics: Solar, wind, battery storage.

🤖 Control Systems

Develop systems that control machines and industrial processes.

Topics: PLC, automation, feedback systems.

🔋 Power Electronics

Conversion and control of electrical power using electronic devices.

Topics: Inverters, converters, rectifiers.

🚗 Electric Vehicles

Work on electric motors, EV batteries and charging technology.

Topics: EV systems, BMS, charging stations.

🏭 Industrial Electrical

Electrical installation, maintenance and industrial equipment.

Topics: Motors, panels, industrial safety.

🧠 Embedded Systems

Combine electronics, programming and electrical hardware.

Topics: Microcontrollers, sensors, C programming.

Electrical Engineering Roadmap

Follow these stages to build your career step by step.

01

Build Your Foundation

Learn Mathematics, Physics, basic electricity, voltage, current, resistance and circuits.

02

Learn Core Subjects

Study electrical machines, power systems, network theory, control systems and measurements.

03

Learn Technical Tools

Practice MATLAB, Simulink, AutoCAD Electrical, ETAP and PLC basics.

04

Build Practical Projects

Create circuits, solar monitoring systems, motor control projects and automation prototypes.

05

Internship & Training

Apply for internships in power plants, electrical companies, automation and renewable energy.

06

Prepare for Jobs

Prepare your resume, technical interview, aptitude, communication and practical knowledge.
