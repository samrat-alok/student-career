ABOUT THE FIELD

What is Emergency Medicine?

Emergency Medicine is a medical specialty focused on the immediate assessment, stabilisation, diagnosis and initial treatment of patients with acute illnesses and injuries. Emergency Physicians work in emergency departments and coordinate with specialists and healthcare teams to provide timely medical care.

🚑 Emergency Care

Initial assessment and treatment of patients arriving with urgent medical conditions.

❤️ Critical Care

Recognise life-threatening conditions and support patient stabilisation.

🩺 Trauma Management

Assess and coordinate care for patients with serious injuries.

⚕️ Medical Emergencies

Manage urgent conditions such as breathing difficulties, severe infections and cardiac emergencies.

EDUCATION PATH

Educational Requirements

01

Class 10

Build a strong foundation in Science, Mathematics and English.

02

Class 11–12: PCB

Choose Physics, Chemistry and Biology and meet the applicable medical entrance eligibility requirements.

03

NEET-UG

Prepare for and qualify NEET-UG according to current medical admission rules.

04

MBBS Degree

Complete a recognised Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery programme.

05

Compulsory Internship

Complete the required clinical internship and medical registration formalities.

06

Postgraduate Admission

Meet the current eligibility criteria and qualify the applicable postgraduate admission process.

07

MD Emergency Medicine / DNB Emergency Medicine

Complete recognised postgraduate specialist training in Emergency Medicine.

08

Advanced Clinical Training

Develop expertise in areas such as critical care, trauma, toxicology or paediatric emergencies through appropriate further training.

09

CHOOSE YOUR INTEREST

Emergency Medicine Specialisations

Trauma Emergency Care

Assessment and initial management of patients with serious injuries.

Cardiac Emergencies

Urgent assessment of heart-related emergencies.

Paediatric Emergency Medicine

Emergency assessment and care for infants and children.

Critical Care

Care of patients with life-threatening illnesses and organ dysfunction.

Emergency Toxicology

Assessment and management of poisoning and toxic exposures.

Disaster Medicine

Medical response planning and care during large-scale emergencies.

Pre-hospital Emergency Care

Emergency medical systems and care before hospital arrival.

Neurological Emergencies

Initial assessment of urgent brain and nervous system conditions.

Emergency Ultrasound

Use of point-of-care ultrasound by appropriately trained clinicians.

Environmental Emergencies

Assessment of heat-related illness, hypothermia and other environmental conditions.

PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Important Skills

Rapid Assessment

Learn structured assessment and prioritisation of patients.

Clinical Decision-Making

Make evidence-based decisions in time-sensitive situations.

Emergency Communication

Communicate clearly with patients, families and medical teams.

Teamwork

Coordinate effectively with nurses, specialists and emergency staff.

Patient Stabilisation

Develop clinical knowledge for initial stabilisation under supervised training.

Stress Management

Maintain professional judgement and composure during emergencies.

Medical Ethics

Respect patient dignity, privacy and informed consent.

Leadership

Coordinate emergency response and work effectively within clinical teams.
