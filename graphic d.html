Design Fundamentals

Understand how visual communication works before mastering software.

  • Elements of design: line, shape, space and texture
  • Principles: contrast, balance, alignment and hierarchy
  • Color theory, typography and composition

FOUNDATION

Learn Design Tools

Start with one accessible tool, then expand according to your goals.

  • Canva: layouts, templates and social graphics
  • Photo editing with Photoshop or alternatives
  • Vector graphics with Illustrator or Inkscape
  • Explore Figma for interface design

Practice: Recreate a poster and design a simple logo.

03
PRACTICE

Develop Practical Skills

Move from copying tutorials to solving creative briefs independently.

  • Logo variations and brand identity systems
  • Social media campaign and advertising creatives
  • Print layouts and export settings
  • File organization, feedback and revisions

Practice: Build a mini brand identity for a fictional business.

04
PORTFOLIO

Build Your Design Portfolio

Show the problem, your creative process, design decisions and final output.

  • Prepare 4–6 well-presented case studies as a starting target
  • Show original work, variations and mockups
  • Explain the audience, brief and design choices
  • Publish selected work on Behance or a personal website

Practice: Create a portfolio featuring your strongest projects.

CAREER PREPARATION

Prepare for Internships

Present your work professionally and learn how design teams operate.

  • Create a one-page resume and portfolio link

  • Search for Graphic Design Intern and Visual Design Intern roles
  • Check role descriptions, eligibility, stipend and deadlines

Practice: Prepare a tailored application for one suitable role.

Explore internships ↗

  • Practise portfolio walkthroughs and design assignments

  • Seek feedback and update your portfolio regularly

09 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I learn graphic design without a design degree?

Yes, a degree is not the only route. Practical skills, a strong portfolio and relevant experience are important, although some employers require specific qualifications.

Do I need to know coding?

Not for most graphic design roles. HTML and CSS can be useful if you later explore web design or collaborate with developers.

Which software should a beginner learn first?

Start with one tool such as Canva or Inkscape, then choose Photoshop, Illustrator or Figma according to your intended specialisation.

How do I get my first internship?

Build a small portfolio of original projects, prepare a tailored resume, search for relevant openings and follow each employer's application instructions.

Will AI replace graphic designers?

AI is changing creative workflows. Develop visual judgement, original concepts, communication, brand thinking and responsible use of AI tools to adapt.

Does completing this page guarantee a job?

No. This page provides learning guidance and planning tools, not an accredited qualification, verified vacancy feed or job guarantee.
