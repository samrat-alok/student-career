01 / INTRODUCTION

What Is Interior Design?

Interior Design is the art and science of planning indoor spaces. Interior designers work on room layouts, furniture, lighting, colors, materials, decoration and functionality to make spaces comfortable, attractive and useful.

🏠 Space Planning

Plan rooms, layouts, furniture placement and movement areas.

💡 Lighting Design

Use natural and artificial lighting to improve spaces.

🎨 Creative Design

Choose colors, materials, textures and decoration styles.
