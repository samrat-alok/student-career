01 / INTRODUCTION

What Is Fashion Design?

Fashion Design is the process of creating clothing, accessories, footwear and lifestyle products. It combines creativity, art, technology, fabric knowledge and business skills. Fashion designers create new styles according to customer needs, culture, trends and market demand.

✏️ Creativity

Develop original designs, sketches, patterns and collections.

🧵 Fabric Knowledge

Understand textiles, materials, colors, prints and stitching.

💻 Digital Design

Use professional software to create digital fashion designs.
