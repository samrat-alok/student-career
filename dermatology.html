EDUCATION PATHWAY

Understand the academic journey required to become a Dermatologist.

Class 10

Build a strong foundation in Science, Mathematics and English. Develop an interest in biology and human health.

Class 11–12

Choose Physics, Chemistry and Biology (PCB). Prepare for the NEET-UG medical entrance examination.

After Class 12

Qualify NEET-UG and secure admission to an eligible medical college for the MBBS programme.

After MBBS

Complete the required internship and registration process, then pursue recognised postgraduate training in Dermatology.

DERMATOLOGY SPECIALISATIONS

Explore different areas of dermatological practice.

Clinical Dermatology

Diagnose and manage common and complex skin diseases and inflammatory skin conditions.

Cosmetic Dermatology

Focus on aesthetic skin treatments and procedures within professional scope and training.

Paediatric Dermatology

Manage skin, hair and nail conditions affecting infants and children.

Dermatopathology

Study skin diseases through microscopic examination and tissue analysis.

Hair and Scalp Disorders

Focus on hair loss, scalp diseases and related conditions.

Dermatosurgery

Learn surgical procedures used to diagnose and treat selected skin conditions.

Laser Dermatology

Explore the supervised use of laser-based technologies for selected dermatological conditions.

Sexually Transmitted Infections

Study the diagnosis, prevention and management of sexually transmitted infections.

ESSENTIAL SKILLS

Medical Knowledge

Understand skin anatomy, physiology, dermatological diseases and treatment principles.

Clinical Diagnosis

Develop skills in examining skin, hair and nail conditions and identifying possible diagnoses.

Dermatoscopy

Learn the principles of examining skin lesions with specialised magnification tools.

Medical Procedures

Develop procedural knowledge through supervised clinical and practical training.

Communication

Explain skin conditions, treatment options and preventive care to patients.

Patient Counselling

Guide patients on treatment plans, skincare routines and follow-up care.

Research Skills

Understand medical research, clinical evidence and dermatological developments.

Professional Ethics

Maintain patient confidentiality, informed consent and responsible clinical practice.

    CAREER & JOB OPPORTUNITIES

    Clinical Dermatologist

    Diagnose and manage skin, hair and nail disorders in eligible clinical settings.

    Consultant Dermatologist

    Provide specialist dermatological consultation in hospitals and healthcare institutions.

    Cosmetic Dermatologist

    Focus on aesthetic dermatology within appropriate qualifications and training.

    Paediatric Dermatologist

    Work with skin conditions affecting children after appropriate specialist training.

    Medical Educator

    Teach medical students and train future healthcare professionals.

    Clinical Researcher

    Participate in research related to skin diseases, medicines and dermatological treatments.
