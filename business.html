      STEP 03

      Naya Business Idea Kaise Dhoondhein?

      Idea sirf unique hona zaroori nahi; uska real customer problem solve karna bhi zaroori hai.

      🔎

      Problems Observe Karo

      Students, families, shops aur local businesses ko daily kis problem ka saamna hai?

      🗣️

      Customers Se Baat Karo

      10–20 potential customers se unki problem, current solution aur budget poochho.

      🧪

      Small Test Karo

      Prototype, sample, landing page ya demo se interest aur demand test karo.

      🔁

      Feedback Se Improve Karo

      Users ki feedback se product ko badlo; phir dobara test karo.

      Business Idea Explorer

      Apni interest category choose karo aur starting idea dekho.

      Try an idea

      Select a category and click “Suggest an Idea”.

      Ye starting suggestions hain, guaranteed profitable business ideas nahi. Launch se pehle customer demand validate karo.

      STEP 04

      Apna Product Launch Kaise Karein?

      Full investment se pehle chhota version launch karke demand test karo.

      1

      Customer & Problem Define Karo

      Kaun kharidega aur tumhara product unki kaunsi problem solve karega?

      2

      Competitor Analysis Karo

      Competitors ki pricing, quality, reviews aur missing features compare karo.

      3

      MVP / Sample Banao

      Product ka minimum useful version banao, jisse main idea test ho sake.

      4

      Pricing & Cost Calculate Karo

      Production, delivery, marketing, taxes aur customer support jaise costs include karo.

      5

      Small Launch Karo

      Limited customers ke saath test launch karke feedback aur real sales measure karo.

      6

      Improve & Expand Karo

      Demand aur unit economics achhe hone par marketing aur production ko scale karo.

      STEP 05

      Product Ko Top 10 Mein Kaise Le Jayein?

      Top 10 ka matlab clear karo: sales ranking, customer ratings, market share ya category popularity?

      01

      Quality

      Product ko reliable aur customer ki need ke mutabik banao.

      02

      Clear Difference

      Customers ko samjhao ki tumhara product competitors se kaise alag hai.

      03

      Fair Pricing

      Customer value aur total costs ko dekhkar sustainable price rakho.

      04

      Brand Identity

      Memorable name, clear message, consistent design aur genuine claims use karo.

      05

      Customer Reviews

      Real feedback maango, complaints solve karo aur fake reviews se bacho.

      06

      Digital Presence

      Useful website, search-friendly content aur relevant social channels banao.

      07

      Marketing

      Customer ke hisaab se content, partnerships aur measured advertising try karo.

      08

      Customer Service

      Fast response, transparent policies aur dependable after-sales support do.

      09

      Track Data

      Sales, repeat purchases, returns, customer acquisition cost aur profit track karo.

      10

      Keep Improving

      Customer feedback aur performance data ke basis par product update karo.

      Important: Top 10 Ki Guarantee Nahi Hoti

      Ranking category, location, time aur platform par depend karti hai. Fake reviews, misleading claims ya sirf ads par kharch karna sustainable growth ki guarantee nahi hai.
