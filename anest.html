ABOUT THE FIELD

What is Anesthesiology?

Anesthesiology is a medical specialty concerned with perioperative patient care, anesthesia, pain management and the monitoring of vital functions during medical and surgical procedures. Anesthesiologists help patients undergo operations safely, assess their medical condition before procedures, provide appropriate anesthesia and monitor recovery.

💉 General Anesthesia

Provide medically supervised anesthesia for appropriate surgical procedures.

🩺 Regional Anesthesia

Specialised techniques that reduce or block sensation in selected body regions.

❤️ Patient Monitoring

Monitor vital signs and assess patient condition throughout procedures.

⚕️ Pain Management

Evaluate and manage acute and chronic pain through appropriate medical care.

CHOOSE YOUR INTEREST

Anesthesiology Specialisations

Cardiac Anesthesia

Specialised anesthesia and perioperative care for patients undergoing heart surgery.

Neuroanesthesia

Perioperative anesthesia care for brain and nervous system procedures.

Paediatric Anesthesia

Anesthesia care for infants, children and adolescents.

Obstetric Anesthesia

Anesthesia and pain relief care related to pregnancy and childbirth.

Critical Care Medicine

Further training focused on patients with serious and life-threatening conditions.

Pain Medicine

Assessment and treatment of acute and chronic pain conditions.

Regional Anesthesia

Specialised techniques for targeted anesthesia and pain control.

Transplant Anesthesia

Perioperative care for patients undergoing organ transplantation.

Thoracic Anesthesia

Specialised anesthesia care for chest and lung surgery.

Ambulatory Anesthesia

Anesthesia services for selected procedures that allow same-day discharge.

PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Important Skills

Patient Assessment

Evaluate medical history and patient condition before procedures.

Airway Management

Develop knowledge of airway assessment and supervised airway-management techniques.

Patient Monitoring

Understand vital-sign monitoring and perioperative patient safety.

Clinical Decision-Making

Make evidence-based decisions and recognise potential complications.

Emergency Response

Learn to recognise and respond to medical emergencies through supervised training.

Communication

Explain procedures and communicate effectively with patients and healthcare teams.

Precision and Focus

Maintain attention to detail during complex clinical procedures.

Medical Ethics

Respect patient safety, privacy, consent and professional responsibilities.
