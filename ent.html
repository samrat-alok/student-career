What is ENT?

ENT, also known as Otorhinolaryngology, is a medical specialty dealing with conditions of the ear, nose, throat, and related structures of the head and neck. Doctors in this specialty are called ENT Specialists or Otorhinolaryngologists.

Ear Care

Diagnosis and treatment of hearing loss, ear infections, balance disorders and other ear-related conditions.

Nose Care

Treatment of sinusitis, nasal allergies, nasal obstruction and other nasal conditions.

Throat Care

Management of tonsil problems, voice disorders, swallowing difficulties and throat diseases.

Head & Neck Care

Diagnosis and treatment of selected head and neck disorders, including tumours and related conditions.

Major ENT Specialisations

Explore different advanced areas within ENT and Otorhinolaryngology.

Otology

Focuses on ear diseases, hearing problems, middle ear disorders and ear surgery.

Neurotology

Deals with complex ear and skull-base conditions involving hearing and balance systems.

Rhinology

Specialises in nasal disorders, sinus diseases, allergies and endoscopic sinus procedures.

Laryngology

Focuses on voice disorders, vocal cord conditions and diseases of the larynx.

Head & Neck Surgery

Deals with selected tumours and surgical conditions of the head and neck.

Paediatric ENT

Provides specialised care for ear, nose and throat conditions in children.

Facial Plastic Surgery

Focuses on reconstructive and selected cosmetic procedures involving the face.

Sleep Medicine

Evaluates and manages selected sleep-related breathing disorders, including obstructive sleep apnoea.

Important Skills to Develop

Medical Knowledge

Understand ear, nose, throat and head and neck anatomy.

Clinical Examination

Develop skills in ear, nose and throat examination under appropriate supervision.

Endoscopic Skills

Learn the principles of nasal and laryngeal endoscopic examination.

Surgical Skills

Develop surgical knowledge and procedural skills through supervised clinical training.

Hearing Assessment

Understand audiology tests and hearing evaluation methods.

Communication

Explain diagnoses and treatment options clearly and respectfully to patients.

Clinical Decision Making

Develop diagnostic reasoning and evidence-based treatment planning.

Ethics & Empathy

Respect patient privacy, informed consent and professional medical ethics.
