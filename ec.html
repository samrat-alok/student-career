FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is Electronics and Communication Engineering?

ECE is an engineering discipline focused on electronic devices, circuits, communication systems, signal processing and related technologies.

Which programming languages are useful in ECE?

C, C++, Python, Embedded C and MATLAB are useful in different electronics and communication specializations.

What is VLSI engineering?

VLSI stands for Very Large Scale Integration. It involves designing integrated circuits that contain large numbers of electronic components on a chip.

What is the difference between embedded systems and IoT?

An embedded system is a computer integrated into a device to perform specific functions. IoT connects devices so that they can exchange information over a network.

Can ECE students work in the IT industry?

Yes. ECE graduates can pursue software, programming, networking, automation and other technology roles when they develop the required skills.

Which ECE specialization should beginners choose?

Start with basic electronics and programming. Then explore embedded systems, VLSI, communication, PCB design or IoT based on your interests and strengths.
