What is Ophthalmology?

Ophthalmology is a branch of medicine that focuses on the anatomy, function, diseases and treatment of the eyes and visual system. An eye specialist is called an Ophthalmologist. Ophthalmologists are qualified medical doctors who can diagnose eye conditions and perform eye surgery after appropriate training.

Eye Diagnosis

Examine vision and identify eye conditions such as cataracts, glaucoma and retinal diseases.

Eye Surgery

Perform specialised eye procedures and surgeries after completing appropriate clinical training.

Vision Care

Evaluate visual problems and provide appropriate medical treatment and follow-up care.

Major Ophthalmology Specialisations

Ophthalmologists can pursue advanced training in different areas of eye care and surgery.

Retina Specialist

Focuses on retinal diseases, diabetic retinopathy, retinal detachment and macular disorders.

Cornea Specialist

Treats corneal diseases, injuries, infections and conditions affecting corneal transparency.

Glaucoma Specialist

Diagnoses and manages glaucoma and conditions involving optic nerve damage.

Paediatric Ophthalmology

Focuses on children's eye health, squint, lazy eye and childhood vision disorders.

Oculoplasty

Deals with eyelid, tear drainage system, orbit and reconstructive eye procedures.

Neuro-Ophthalmology

Studies vision problems related to the brain, optic nerve and nervous system.

Cataract Surgery

Focuses on cataract diagnosis and surgical treatment to improve vision.

Refractive Surgery

Specialises in procedures such as LASIK and other techniques for correcting refractive errors.

Important Skills to Develop

Medical Knowledge

Understand eye anatomy, physiology and common eye diseases.

Clinical Examination

Learn visual acuity testing and eye examination techniques.

Surgical Skills

Develop precision and procedural knowledge through supervised training.

Diagnostic Skills

Learn to interpret appropriate eye investigations and clinical findings.

Communication

Explain diagnoses and treatment options clearly to patients.

Attention to Detail

Build accuracy and concentration during examinations and procedures.

Problem Solving

Develop clinical reasoning and evidence-based decision-making.

Ethics & Empathy

Respect patient privacy, informed consent and professional ethics.

    Career Opportunities

    Ophthalmologist

    Diagnose and treat diseases of the eyes and visual system.

    Consultant Eye Specialist

    Provide specialist eye care in hospitals and eye clinics.

    Retina Specialist

    Manage retinal conditions after appropriate advanced training.

    Eye Surgeon

    Perform eye surgeries within the scope of specialist training.

    Medical Educator

    Teach medical students and contribute to clinical education.

    Clinical Researcher

    Participate in research related to eye diseases and vision care.

    Eye Hospital Specialist

    Work in specialised eye hospitals and vision-care centres.

    Private Practice

    Establish or join a clinic after meeting professional requirements.
