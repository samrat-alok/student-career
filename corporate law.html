CAREER OVERVIEW

What is Corporate Law?

Understand how lawyers help companies follow laws and manage business transactions.

🏢

Corporate Lawyer

A legal professional who advises businesses on company law, contracts, transactions and legal risks.

📑

Legal Agreements

Work with commercial agreements, employment contracts, NDAs and business documentation.

📊

Business Compliance

Help organizations understand regulatory requirements, governance and compliance obligations.

🤝

Business Transactions

Support mergers, acquisitions, investments and other corporate transactions.

Important: Corporate legal jobs have different eligibility requirements. Some roles require an LL.B. and professional qualifications, while certain contract or compliance support roles may have other eligibility criteria.
