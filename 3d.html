01 / INTRODUCTION

What Is 3D Design?

3D Design is the process of creating digital objects, characters, products, buildings and environments in three dimensions: width, height and depth. Designers use specialized software to model, texture, light and render objects for industries such as gaming, films, architecture, manufacturing, advertising and product visualization.

🧊 3D Modelling

Create digital objects, characters, furniture, machines and environments.

🎨 Visual Creativity

Develop materials, textures, colours, shapes and realistic designs.

⚙️ Technology

Use CAD tools, rendering engines and professional 3D software.

03 / DESIGN PIPELINE

How Is a 3D Model Created?

Understand the professional 3D design workflow.

01

Concept & Reference

Plan the object and collect reference images and measurements.

02

Blockout

Build the basic shapes and proportions of the model.

03

Modelling

Create the detailed 3D geometry of the object.

04

Sculpting

Add fine details, shapes and organic surface features where needed.

05

UV Mapping

Prepare the model surface for accurate texture placement.

06

Texturing & Materials

Apply colour, roughness, metallic and other material properties.

07

Lighting & Camera

Set up the scene, lighting and camera composition.

08

Rendering & Export

Generate the final image, video or 3D model file.
