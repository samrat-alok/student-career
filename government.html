        FITNESS & ELIGIBILITY

        Physical Training Guide

        Physical tests are required only for selected posts. Check the exact recruitment notice.

        🏃

        Running & Stamina

        • Begin with a comfortable walk and light jog.
        • Increase distance gradually and include recovery days.
        • Practise the exact event only after checking the official standard.
        💪

        Strength & Mobility

        • Practise bodyweight squats, modified push-ups and core exercises.
        • Warm up before exercise and cool down afterward.
        • Build technique before increasing intensity.
        📏

        PET / PST Standards

        • Check height, chest, running, jumps or other applicable tests.
        • Standards can vary by post, category, sex and recruitment rules.
        • Follow the current official notification rather than general estimates.

        Beginner Weekly Fitness Routine

        Day 1Easy walk / jog
        Day 2Light strength + mobility
        Day 3Recovery walk or rest
        Day 4Easy walk / jog
        Day 5Light strength + mobility
        Day 6Comfortable endurance practice
        Day 7Rest and recovery

        This is a general beginner routine, not an official PET training standard. Adjust it to your fitness level, avoid training through pain and seek medical advice if needed.

        Medical Test Information

        Medical examination requirements depend on the post. They may include general physical examination, vision or hearing assessment and other job-specific checks as notified by the recruiting authority.

        • Read the official medical standards for your specific post.
        • Bring required documents and previous medical records if requested.
        • Only the authorized medical board can determine fitness under the applicable rules.
        • Do not self-diagnose or rely on a generic website to determine eligibility.
